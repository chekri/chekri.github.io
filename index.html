About Me

Hello, I am Siva Prasad Chakri Dhanakoti, a researcher working at the confluence of Applied Mathematics, Mechanics, Physics and Engineering. Currently, I am working as a post-doctoral researcher in the University of Trento, Italy under the collaboration of Prof. Francesco Dal Corso and Prof. Davide Bigoni. I am interested in modelling mechanical systems especially slender structures and investigating their nonlinear behavior. These nonlinearities can be harnessed in innovative technologies of metamaterials and soft robots as well as gain some insights into complex phenomena found in nature and biology.

Research

Interests: Calculus of Variations, Solid Mechanics, Nonlinear Dynamics, Optimization and Numerical Methods

Current Research: The dynamics of slender structures/metastructures

PhD research: 3D slender structures and instabilities
